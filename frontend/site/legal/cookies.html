Cookie Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026 · Version {{TERMS_VERSION}}

Reality ASM uses as little browser storage as it can. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, so there's nothing to opt out of.

Cookies

NamePurposeLifetime
rasm_sessionKeeps you signed in. Strictly necessary. HttpOnly, so page scripts can't read it.30 days, or until you close the browser if you untick "Keep me signed in"
rasm_oauthProtects the "Continue with Google" sign-in from forgery. Only set while you sign in with Google.10 minutes

Local storage

The app also keeps a few settings in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload: that you've seen the cookie notice, your own engine and satellite designs in the Spaceflight Lab, and small hand-offs between pages (for example the date to launch on when you jump from the Solar System to the Spaceflight Lab). They never leave your device unless you save something to your history.

Managing them

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser's settings. If you remove rasm_session you'll be signed out.
