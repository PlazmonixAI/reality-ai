We don't use advertising, tracking pixels or third-party analytics, and we don't sell or rent personal data.

Who else processes your data

  • AI provider. When you ask the AI analyst a question, the question, the recent conversation and a compact summary of the simulation on your screen are sent to the language-model provider we use (NVIDIA NIM, Groq or xAI, depending on configuration) to write the answer. Don't put personal information in AI questions.
  • NASA imagery. Satellite photos in the space company load public Earth imagery from NASA's GIBS service directly in your browser. NASA sees your IP address and the map area requested, as with any website.
  • Hosting and email. Our hosting provider stores the database and servers. If password-reset email is switched on, our email provider delivers those messages.
  • Google. Only if you choose "Continue with Google".

These providers may process data outside India. Where they do, we rely on their contractual and legal safeguards.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: Account settings → Download my data gives you everything we hold about you as JSON.
  • Correct it: change your name in Account settings, or write to us for anything else.
  • Delete it: Account settings → Delete account removes your account, history, fleet, photos and sessions straight away. Backups roll over within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent, complain or ask questions: write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You can also complain to your data-protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India; in the EU or UK, your local supervisory authority).

We rely on your consent and on performing our agreement with you (the Terms) to process your data, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Sessions use random tokens in HttpOnly cookies, and only a hash of each token is stored. Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS in production. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

The Service is for people aged 13 and over. Users under 18 should have a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data in a meaningful way, we'll update this page and tell you by email or in the app before the change applies.
