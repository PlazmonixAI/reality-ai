About Reality ASM

A research-simulation engine by Subham Agarwal and Plazmonix AI.

Reality ASM (Advanced Simulation Machine) started from a simple rule: every number on the screen has to be computed by a real physical model, never made up for the animation. The browser draws; a Python engine built on NumPy, SciPy and SymPy does the work, and each of its 130+ tools is checked against textbook values before it ships.

What you can do today

  • Build rockets from real stages and fly them to orbit, the Moon and back in the Spaceflight Lab.
  • Run a space company: launch real satellites like Cartosat-3 or EOS-05, keep them flying against drag, take pictures of Earth and send probes to Mars and Jupiter.
  • Explore the Solar System on any date, then zoom out to the stars, the Milky Way and the observable universe.
  • Try 78 simulations across physics, chemistry and mathematics, with an AI analyst next to each one.
  • Take on mission challenges that the server checks for real.
