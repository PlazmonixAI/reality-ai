What to test

  1. Create an account (use the invite code if the team gave you one), sign out and sign back in.
  2. Spaceflight Lab: pick PSLV-XL, launch, and watch the strap-on boosters burn out and drop away.
  3. Start in low orbit, open the map, and try "To Ap" and the quick save (K) and load (L).
  4. Mission Control: found a company, launch Cartosat-3, take a photo in daylight, and fire a small burn.
  5. Send a probe to Mars with "Find launch windows", then look for it in the Solar System.
  6. Challenges: finish "First orbit". Then open History and continue an old flight.
  7. Anything odd? Use "Send feedback" in the account menu, or write to contact@plazmonixai.in.
