Open the app

The simulations, the AI, ASM Teach and accounts run on the backend, which is not live yet. Once it is on Render, put its address in beta_app_url (cloudflare/site.config.json), rebuild and push. The buttons for the app and for ASM Teach then appear here, and the Backend card above turns green.

What to test once the app is live

  1. Create an account, sign out and sign back in.
  2. Spaceflight Lab: pick PSLV-XL, launch, and watch the strap-on boosters burn out and drop away.
  3. Solar System: use the mission clock to run forward a year, then press Now.
  4. Mission Control: found a company, launch Cartosat-3, take a photo in daylight and fire a small burn.
  5. ASM Teach: sign up a school with Google, add a teacher, sign in as the teacher.
  6. On the board, write sin^2 x + cos^2 x = 1 in the Equation Lab, then say "T equals 2 pi root L by g" with Listen.
  7. Show a PDF from Files and a YouTube video in the same lesson, then save the lesson.
  8. Anything odd? Write to contact@plazmonixai.in.
